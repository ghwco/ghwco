Logo options

Same palette and typography in both — the only variable is the mountain treatment. Shown large, at nav size, and at 20px favicon size, on light and dark. The 20px row is the one that matters most: it's how the mark appears in a browser tab, an Instagram avatar, and an email signature.

Option A

With snowcapIn use

The one currently live on the site. The white notch reads clearly at large sizes and says "Colorado" immediately — it's the first detail to soften when the mark gets small, but it holds up better than I expected at 20px. Staying with this for now; B and C are here as alternates if we revisit.

Option B

No snowcapAlternate
