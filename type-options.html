My read

If the goal is warm but high-end, EB Garamond or Instrument Serif. Garamond is the safer, softer choice and pairs beautifully with the terracotta palette. Instrument is the more striking one and would push the whole site a notch more editorial, which suits the photography you now have. Lora is the pragmatic middle. I'd skip Playfair here purely because every second wellness site uses it. Swapping is one line: --serif in assets/brand.css, plus the Google Fonts link in each page head.
