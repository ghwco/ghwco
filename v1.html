Welcome

A practice built around the person in the room.

Golden Hour Wellness Colorado, LLC was founded by Shayla Martinez-O’Brien, a Licensed Professional Counselor and Licensed Addiction Counselor with 11 years of clinical practice. What started as one clinician is now a small, hand-picked team.

We believe true healing comes from an increased ability to tolerate challenging emotion, paired with a clearer understanding of your own value system. That work is slow, human, and worth it — and you don’t have to do it alone.

“You can expect a co-pilot on your journey.” Shayla Martinez-O’Brien, LPC, LAC · Founder

How we help

Specialized care, without the clinical chill.

Every therapist here brings a different lane of expertise. We’ll help you find the right fit on your first call — even if that fit turns out to be somewhere else.

Trauma & EMDR

Eye Movement Desensitization and Reprocessing alongside other trauma-focused approaches — available in person and via telehealth.

Anxiety & Depression

Solution-oriented, CBT-informed work on how mood, thoughts, and behavior shape one another — plus practical tools you can actually use.

Substance Use & Recovery

Licensed addiction counseling for people living with substance use disorders, or anyone feeling stuck and disconnected from themselves.

Relationships & Life Transitions

Family stress, work stress, big pivots. Support for navigating major change with more clarity and a stronger sense of purpose.
